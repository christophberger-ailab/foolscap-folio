# Handbook

This is the `index.md` of the topic folder `handbook/`: the book's **preliminaries**. Its front matter's `title` and `subtitle` go on the book's cover. In a printed book, the text of this page comes after the cover and before the table of contents, which makes it the place for a foreword, a copyright notice or a word on how to read the book.

The pages and folders next to this file are the book's **sections**, in the order their `order:` fields give: [First steps](/handbook/first-steps.md) and [Writing](/handbook/writing/).
